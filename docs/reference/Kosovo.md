# Kosovo conflict data

Four-list capture-history data for 4,400 documented killings during the
Kosovo conflict between 20 March and 22 June 1999. The lists are
exhumations (`EXH`), the American Bar Association Central and East
European Law Initiative (`ABA`), the Organization for Security and
Co-operation in Europe (`OSCE`), and Human Rights Watch (`HRW`). All 15
observable capture histories have positive count.

## Usage

``` r
Kosovo
```

## Format

A data frame with 15 rows and 5 columns. The first four columns are
binary list-membership indicators. Column `Frequency` gives the number
of cases having each capture history.

## References

Ball, P., Betts, W., Scheuren, F., Dudukovich, J. and Asher, J. (2002).
Killings and refugee flow in Kosovo, March–June 1999. American
Association for the Advancement of Science. Report to the International
Criminal Tribunal for the Former Yugoslavia.
