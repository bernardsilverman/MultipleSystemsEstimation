# MultipleSystemsEstimation

Methods for multiple systems estimation using Poisson log-linear models,
with particular attention to sparse capture data, model selection and
its associated uncertainty, and Bayesian thresholding of interaction
effects.

The package implements methodology from Silverman (2020), Chan,
Silverman and Vincent (2021), and Silverman, Chan and Vincent (2024).

## References

Silverman, B. W. (2020). Multiple-systems analysis for the
quantification of modern slavery: classical and Bayesian approaches.
*Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series A*, 183, 691–736.

Chan, L., Silverman, B. W. and Vincent, K. (2021). Multiple systems
estimation for sparse capture data: inferential challenges when there
are nonoverlapping lists. *Journal of the American Statistical
Association*, 116, 129–143.

Silverman, B. W., Chan, L. and Vincent, K. (2024). Multiple systems
estimation with model selection and uncertainty. *Statistics and
Computing*.
